Dexter personal tools · Terms of use

Privacy policy

Dexter personal tools is operated by Alex Rattray for himself and the known teammates and agents he authorizes. There is no public signup.

Last updated: September 20, 2026.

Google data and permitted actions

Depending on the permissions granted and the task, Dexter accesses account identity, mail and attachments, calendar events, contacts, and Drive files and metadata.

This information supports requested or specifically authorized tasks, such as searches, summaries, document preparation, and schedule coordination. Dexter performs changes, including messages or updates to events and files, only within the instructions and permissions granted.

Google data use is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Agent processing and sharing

Requested data may be sent to configured OpenAI or Anthropic agent environments to perform the task. Alex and authorized teammates or agents may access task information within their authorized work.

An authorized action may also send information to its intended recipients or destinations, such as an email recipient or a shared document. Provider processing and retention depend on the services and account settings in use.

Credentials and stored information

Google credentials are stored in 1Password and authorized environments. They let the tools access the connected account within its granted permissions.

Task information and outputs may remain in notes, generated files, agent conversation histories, or authorized environments. Retention varies by workflow and provider configuration; there is no single automatic deletion period for all copies.

Revoke access or request deletion

Open your Google Account connections, select Dexter's connection, and remove its access. Google's instructions explain how to review and remove access.

Revocation stops further access through that Google grant. It does not automatically delete information already copied into task records, outputs, or provider systems.

To request deletion of stored credentials or task information, contact alex@pny.bike. Alex can identify the relevant stores and available deletion steps, including any provider retention limits.

These public pages

These information pages use Alex's existing website hosting and analytics. Those services may receive ordinary browser and page-request information when you visit.

Questions and updates

Contact Alex at alex@pny.bike with privacy questions or concerns. Changes to this policy will appear here with an updated date.
