Dexter personal tools

Private tools for Alex Rattray and the known teammates and agents he authorizes.

Alex operates Dexter to help with personal and team tasks. There is no public signup.

What Dexter does

With the account holder's permission, Dexter reads approved Google mail, calendar, contacts, and Drive data. It can find information, summarize correspondence, prepare documents, and help coordinate schedules.

Dexter also performs specifically authorized actions, such as sending a message or updating an event or file. Access depends on the permissions granted to the connected account. A read-only connection does not permit changes.

How it handles data

Requested data may be processed in configured OpenAI or Anthropic agent environments. Credentials are stored in 1Password and authorized environments. The privacy policy explains access, processing, storage, and removal.

Contact and access
